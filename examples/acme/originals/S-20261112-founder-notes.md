# Strategy workshop, 12 November 2026

We sell only to independent restaurants with one to three sites. Groups asked for multi-site reporting and we declined.

The product is one plan: shift planning with gap covering, priced per site per month.

All customers come through the self-serve trial; we have no sales team.

Payroll export goes through Paynest, the only payroll provider we integrate with.

The team is six people: four engineers, one designer and one founder on support.
