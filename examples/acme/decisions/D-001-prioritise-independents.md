# D-001 Prioritise independent restaurants

## Context
We must choose a segment for the next three years ([[framing#^q01]]).

## Decision
Prioritise independents with 1 to 3 sites, with gap covering as the core.

## Alternatives discarded
- Groups: they compete with bundled ERPs.

## Review signals
- Trial-to-paid conversion below 8%.
- Evidence that most independents already use a shift app.
