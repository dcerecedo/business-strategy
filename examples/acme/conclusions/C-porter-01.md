# Substitutes are strong wherever an ERP is already in place

## Fact
A competitor bundles shift planning in its ERP from 39 EUR per site per month.

## Interpretation
For anyone already paying for that ERP, our product competes with a marginal price of zero.

## Implication
Our price is only defensible in segments without an ERP: independents.
