# Covering last-minute gaps is the unsolved job for independents

## Fact
Most independents plan in spreadsheets and managers name last-minute changes as their main pain.

## Interpretation
The job is not planning the week but finding a replacement within hours.

## Implication
For this segment the product is sold on gap covering, not on the rota.
